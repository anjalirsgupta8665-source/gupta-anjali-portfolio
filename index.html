02 About A short introduction

Curious by default, precise by practice. I turn messy ideas into calm, considered digital experiences.

I'm Anjali — a creative developer and B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Shri Ramswaroop Memorial University. Most of my learning happens at the intersection of design and intelligence: clean interfaces on one side, algorithms that actually learn on the other.

Off-screen, I collect small rituals — sketching interface ideas, documenting everyday photographic light, and reading about emerging AI with too much enthusiasm. I believe good software should feel like good conversation: unhurried, clear, and a little surprising.
